Additional Insights for Source Reliability

A. Net New MAPE vs Overall MAPE

  • Net New MAPE: 22.2% vs Overall MAPE: 29.1%
  • Sources agree better on future projects than existing facilities
  • This suggests both sources are tracking similar announcements, with differences in how they estimate capacity

B. Coverage Asymmetry

  • 81.6% of SA's net new sites appear in DCH
  • 54.7% of DCH's net new sites appear in SA
  • Implication: SA is more selective/focused — almost everything SA tracks, DCH also tracks
  • DCH has broader but shallower coverage — many sites SA doesn't track (likely smaller colo)
  • When SA has a site, it's likely validated by DCH (high confirmation rate)

C. Capacity Bias Interpretation

  • SA reports +39.5% higher capacity on net new sites
  • Combined with SA's better ground truth accuracy (11.9% vs 17.6% MAPE), this suggests SA is capturing planned expansions that DCH hasn't recorded yet, not over-estimating

📖 Methodology & Definitions

How This Comparison Was Performed

Records from the gold_buildings_full layer are filtered by source (SA vs DCH), then spatially matched using the Haversine formula with a 500m threshold. When multiple potential matches exist, the closest spatial match is selected. Capacity comparisons use the full_capacity_mw field.

Statistical Definitions

Metric Definition Interpretation
MAPE Mean Absolute Percentage Error = mean(|SA - DCH| / max(SA, DCH)) × 100 0-10%: Excellent, 10-20%: Good, 20-35%: Moderate, 35-50%: Significant, >50%: Poor
Bias % Systematic bias = mean(SA - DCH) / mean(DCH) × 100 Positive = SA reports higher capacity on average; Negative = DCH reports higher
CV Coefficient of Variation = std(delta) / mean(|delta|) × 100 Lower CV indicates more consistent disagreements (easier to calibrate)
Pearson r Correlation coefficient between SA and DCH capacity values >0.9: Strong, 0.7-0.9: Moderate, <0.7: Weak linear relationship
Significant Conflict Matched pair with >20% capacity delta OR >10 MW absolute difference These records require manual review and reconciliation
⚠️ Important Note: This comparison uses spatial matching only. Two facilities at the same location may represent different buildings or expansions. Company name matching is performed separately and may not always align due to naming variations between sources.
